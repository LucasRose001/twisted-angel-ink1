Before you come in

How much will my tattoo cost?

It depends on size, detail and placement. Send your idea through the booking form and Charity will give you a price range before anything is booked.

Do I need a deposit?

Yes. A deposit holds your appointment and comes off the price of your tattoo. Ask Charity about rescheduling if something comes up.

Do you take walk-ins?

Charity works by appointment. Message her on Facebook to ask about any last-minute openings.

What should I bring?

Valid photo ID, something to eat or drink, and clothes that make the spot easy to reach.

How do I look after a new tattoo?

Keep it clean, wash gently with unscented soap, pat dry, and use a thin layer of the aftercare product Charity recommends. Skip pools, hot tubs and direct sun until it has healed. She'll go over everything at your appointment.
